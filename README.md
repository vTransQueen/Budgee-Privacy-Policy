# Budgee-Privacy-Policy

Budgee does not collect, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and nothing is sent to the developer.

Your budgets, transactions, photos and files stay on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, Budgee syncs them through your own private iCloud (CloudKit) account and stores automatic backups in your iCloud Drive. The developer cannot access that data.

Photos and files are attached only when you choose them, or when you share them to Budgee through the share sheet.

You can delete your data at any time by deleting budgets in the app or deleting the app and its iCloud data in Settings.
