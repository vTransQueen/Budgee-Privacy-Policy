Budgee Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Overview

Budgee is a personal budgeting app. Your privacy matters: Budgee does not collect your data, does not track you, and contains no advertising or third-party analytics. The developer never receives or has access to your information.

Data We Collect

None. Budgee does not send your budgets, transactions, categories, photos, or files to the developer or to any third party.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your budget data is stored on your device. Budgee makes no network connections of its own.

iCloud Sync

If you are signed into iCloud, Budgee uses Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) to sync your budgets and settings between your devices. This data is stored in your private iCloud account, is governed by Apple's privacy policy, and is not accessible to the developer. If you are not signed into iCloud, your data stays only on your device.

Photos & Files

If you attach photos or files to a transaction, Budgee only accesses the specific items you choose. Attachments are stored with your budget data and never shared with anyone else.

Share Extension

When you share a file to Budgee from another app, it is passed to Budgee on your device through a shared app container. It is not sent anywhere else.

Backups

Budgee's backups are stored on your device. You control when they are created and deleted.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete your budget data at any time from within the app. Deleting the app removes its data from your device. To remove data synced to iCloud, use Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage on your device.

Children's Privacy

Budgee does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here and in the app along with a new effective date.
